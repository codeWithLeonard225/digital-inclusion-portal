Workplace Communications

Develop professional writing habits, document processing skills, and online meeting etiquette.

Lesson 1: Email Etiquette

Professional emails require clear subject lines, respectful greetings, concise message bodies, and structured signatures. Always proofread before sending.

Lesson 2: Professional Communication

Clear communication builds trust in remote and in-office teams. Focus on active listening, timely responses, and concise updates during ongoing projects.

Lesson 3: Creating Accessible Documents

When creating Word documents, PDFs, presentations, or other digital documents, make sure they can be easily understood and accessed by people with different abilities.

  • Use clear headings and proper document structure.
  • Use readable font sizes and good colour contrast.
  • Add alternative text to meaningful images.
  • Use descriptive link text instead of "click here".
  • Make sure the document can be navigated using a keyboard.

Lesson 4: Online Meetings

When participating in video calls (Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet), join on time, keep your microphone muted when not speaking, and use chat tools appropriately.
