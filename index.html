ABOUT THE PORTAL

Building a More Inclusive Digital Experience

The Digital Inclusion Portal is designed to provide persons with disabilities with easier access to useful digital information and opportunities.

Through accessible learning resources, employment information and built-in accessibility tools, the portal aims to reduce barriers that users may experience when accessing online information.
